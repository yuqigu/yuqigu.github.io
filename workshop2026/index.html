Statistical Foundations of Latent Structure and Representation Learning

November 4–5, 2026

Department of Statistics, Columbia University · New York City

Registration and poster submissions are closed.

About

This two-day workshop brings together researchers in statistics, machine learning, and data science to explore the statistical foundations of latent structure and representation learning, including identifiability, statistical efficiency, uncertainty quantification, computation, causality, and interpretability.
